Speaking & workshops

Talks that feel like a supportive class, not a lecture. They're built for teams that are stretched thin, and for neurodivergent and creative people who need practical tools, not more pressure.

Core framework: The Aligned Success Framework

SelfIdentity, story, values and direction
RootsCapacity, energy, rhythm, systems and support
ExpressionVoice, communication, content and contribution
ExpansionVisibility, leadership, revenue and impact
K1

Aligned Success at Work: Self · Roots · Expression · Expansion

Why more pressure and more visibility often bring less freedom, and a simple way to find where a team's success is fragile.
